# Part 1A — Harness Log: Smart Todo CLI

> **To be written by the student.** Under the course AI policy the harness-log entries are the student's own work and are intentionally not drafted by the AI coding agent.
>
> The brief requires this log to show:
>
> 1. the initial coding-agent generation and the relevant task and specification context;
> 2. each harness execution and its outcome;
> 3. any genuine failure-and-repair cycle that occurred during development;
> 4. the failure evidence supplied back to the coding agent;
> 5. what changed after repair, and whether the same checks passed on re-execution;
> 6. the final human ACCEPT or REJECT decision, known limitations and justification.
>
> Evidence to cite is under `evidence/` (`initial-generation/`, `final-generation/`, `harness-results.json`) and in the git history (commit IDs `1A-009` to `1A-012`).
