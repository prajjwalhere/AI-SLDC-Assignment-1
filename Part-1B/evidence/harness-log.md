# Part 1B — Harness Log: Natural-Language Reminder Agent

> **To be written by the student.** Under the course AI policy the harness-log entries are the student's own work and are intentionally not drafted by the AI coding agent.
>
> The brief requires this log to show:
>
> 1. the model and configuration information needed to interpret the evaluation (no secrets);
> 2. the evaluation threshold and the scoring method as specified before the final evaluation;
> 3. the harness results, including the structural, property/invariant and semantic/quality outcomes;
> 4. meaningful failures encountered and the evidence supplied to the coding agent;
> 5. the repair made to the application, prompt, parser or guardrail, and the resulting re-evaluation outcome;
> 6. the final human ACCEPT or REJECT decision, known limitations and residual risks.
>
> Evidence to cite is under `evidence/` (`initial-generation/`, `final-generation/`, `eval-results.json`) and in the git history (commit IDs `1B-010` to `1B-013`).
